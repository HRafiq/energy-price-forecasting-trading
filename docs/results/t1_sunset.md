# T1 follow-up: do battery losses follow sunset rather than the clock?

Generated by `python -m src.health.experiments.t1_sunset`. Production model, median dispatch, 730 validation days from 2024-06-01 to 2026-05-31. The gap to perfect foresight, €16,424, is split into Shapley shares twice: by the saved T1 clock blocks and by windows anchored to sunrise and sunset at the centre of Germany (51.1634°N, 10.4477°E), boundaries rounded to the whole hour. The per-day gaps of the two splits agree to within €0.00, so they divide the same loss. A Shapley share depends on how the periods are grouped, so the two splits are two readings of the same loss, not one measured twice. Both sample fix orders on every day; that noise is independent across days and is inside the intervals below.

On 70 days, in months 6, 7, sunset rounds to 22:00 local and the window after it is cut at the end of the delivery day, so the sunset window holds five hours, not six. Those days carry 38.7% of the June to September gap. The cut lowers the summer sunset share, which works against the hypothesis.

## Added after the run: what the criteria can and cannot show

The criteria compare point estimates. Moving-block bootstrap, 7-day blocks, 5,000 draws:

| pre_sunset, June to September minus October to May | +20.8 | +8.8 to +36.1 |
| post_sunset, June to September minus October to May | -27.8 | -44.1 to -14.4 |
| clock 15-17, June to September minus October to May | -15.7 | -28.2 to -2.9 |
| clock 18-20, June to September minus October to May | +19.6 | +5.2 to +39.9 |

Criterion 1 cannot separate the hypotheses: the seasonal difference of the sunset window and of the equally wide clock window both have intervals that include no difference and large ones.

Criterion 2 passes on the estimate, but the interval includes zero: the data cannot tell the sunset window from the clock window.

Seasonal shifts clear of zero, June to September minus October to May: pre_sunset +20.8 points (+8.8 to +36.1); post_sunset -27.8 points (-44.1 to -14.4); clock 15-17 -15.7 points (-28.2 to -2.9); clock 18-20 +19.6 points (+5.2 to +39.9). In June to September the loss sits later on the clock (from 15:00 to 17:59 into 18:00 to 20:59) and earlier against sunset (from after sunset to before it).
