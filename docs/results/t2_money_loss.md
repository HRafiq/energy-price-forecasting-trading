# T2 follow-up: a forecaster trained on the money

Generated by `python -m src.health.experiments.t2_money_loss`. The production model's q50 is the base; `money_point` adds 100 trees boosted on the SPO+ loss at learning rate 5, refit every 28 days as the production walk-forward does, over 730 validation days from 2024-06-01 to 2026-05-31. Both arms value both legs of dispatch at their point and settle at the realised prices for a 1 MW / 2 MWh battery with €8 wear. The control reproduces the saved validation profit to within €0.01, and every refit base reproduces the saved q50 exactly.

Criterion, fixed before the run: adopt `money_point` only if the mean daily profit difference against `median_forecast` has a 95% moving-block bootstrap interval (7-day blocks, 5,000 draws) entirely above zero.

## Verdict

`money_point` is adopted: the whole interval is above zero. Over the 730 days it made €2,138 against median dispatch, a difference of +2.93 (+1.53 to +4.74) € a day.

## Where the gain sits

## Against a cheap rival

The rival adds to q50 its mean residual per local hour over the last 42 training days, refit on the same calendar. Against median dispatch it made -2.43 (-3.80 to -1.07) € a day, with an MAE of 17.39 €/MWh; the money arm beat it by +5.36 (+3.81 to +7.42) € a day. The gain is not reducible to a simple hour-of-day bias correction.
