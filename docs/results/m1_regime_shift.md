# M1 regime shift experiment

Generated by `python -m src.health.experiments.m1_regime_shift`. Prices and
errors in €/MWh, P&L in €.

## Setup

Frozen before running and not tuned on results.

* Model: `LightGBMConformalModel`, default parameters, 730 training days, 42 calibration days, every feature group except weather (archived weather forecasts start in March 2024).
* Target days 2021-01-01 to 2023-12-31, 1,095 days, forecast walk-forward at 11:40 on the day before delivery through the information set. The hold-out, from 2026-06-01, is not read.
* Arms, on identical days:
  * `frozen`: fitted once before the first target day, never refitted (180 s).
  * `quarterly`: refitted every 91 days (686 s).
  * `monthly`: refitted every 28 days, the production cadence (1,269 s).
  * `naive_previous_day`: previous day's prices with hourly error quantiles, for context (14 s).
* Scores per day: share of periods inside the 90% (q05 to q95) and 50% (q25 to q75) central intervals, mean pinball loss over the seven quantiles, MAE of q50. A spike day has any price above €200. Period and rolling figures are means over days, each day weighted equally.
* Trading: the reference battery, 1 MW / 2 MWh, 90% round trip, €8 per MWh discharged, at most 2 cycles per day, dispatched on each arm's median forecast and settled at realised prices. Capture is that P&L over perfect foresight's, which is the same for every arm.

## The 2022 gas crisis quarters

* 2022Q1: mean price €185, highest €700, 65 spike days, TTF gas €99/MWh. frozen covered 6.9% (MAE 116, capture 21.9%); quarterly covered 89.5% (MAE 53, capture 58.4%); monthly covered 83.2% (MAE 51, capture 72.8%); naive_previous_day covered 86.7% (MAE 62, capture 53.0%).
* 2022Q2: mean price €187, highest €500, 81 spike days, TTF gas €100/MWh. frozen covered 5.5% (MAE 122, capture 13.2%); quarterly covered 92.6% (MAE 38, capture 85.5%); monthly covered 86.3% (MAE 42, capture 83.9%); naive_previous_day covered 88.7% (MAE 45, capture 71.3%).
* 2022Q3: mean price €376, highest €871, 91 spike days, TTF gas €203/MWh. frozen covered 2.4% (MAE 305, capture 1.9%); quarterly covered 47.7% (MAE 84, capture 80.3%); monthly covered 67.7% (MAE 70, capture 85.8%); naive_previous_day covered 84.6% (MAE 79, capture 79.0%).
* 2022Q4: mean price €193, highest €665, 60 spike days, TTF gas €125/MWh. frozen covered 10.1% (MAE 126, capture 41.2%); quarterly covered 98.4% (MAE 41, capture 70.3%); monthly covered 83.9% (MAE 46, capture 82.0%); naive_previous_day covered 88.5% (MAE 52, capture 74.2%).

The frozen model's worst 2022 quarter was 2022Q3, at 2.4% coverage against the 90% target; in the same quarter the quarterly arm covered 47.7% and the monthly arm 67.7%.

Why the frozen arm fell behind: it was fitted once, on prices from 2019-01-02 to 2020-12-31 with mean €34, 99th percentile €73 and highest €200.04. Over the target days its median forecast peaked at €100.35 and its q95 at €138, while prices reached €871. Tree ensembles such as LightGBM predict from values learned on their training data and do not extrapolate beyond them, and here the conformal ranges add errors from the last 42 training days, which is consistent with this model's forecasts staying near its training prices while the market moved far above them. The refitted arms, trained on more recent prices, covered many more of those periods.

## Against the expectation recorded before running

The expectation recorded before anything was run was frozen coverage near 58% and quarterly refits recovering to 86% to 90%. These were expectations, not results.

* Measured frozen 90% coverage: 25.7% over all target days, 6.2% in 2022, 2.4% in its lowest quarter, 2022Q3. That differs from the expectation.
* Measured quarterly 90% coverage: 77.1% over all target days, 82.0% in 2022. That is outside the expected range.
