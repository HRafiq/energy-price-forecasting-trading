# Phase 3 trading strategies

Generated by `python -m src.trading.run_strategies`. Forecasts: `lightgbm_conformal`, walk-forward over the validation window, issued at 11:40 on the day before delivery; no model was retrained. Each schedule is committed before the 12:00 gate and settled at the realised day-ahead price, with the battery as a price taker. Before 1 October 2025 schedules follow hourly products.

Battery: 1 MW / 2 MWh, 90% round-trip efficiency, €8 per MWh discharged, every day starting and ending at 50% state of charge, at most 2 cycles per day.

Target days 2024-06-01 to 2026-05-31, 730 days, 0 skipped. The hold-out, from 2026-06-01, is not used.
