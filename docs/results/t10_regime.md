### The confounder named in the plan, and why it cannot be removed

The plan meant to compare the hourly part of `recent` against the wholly hourly `crisis`, to tell the product change apart from the regime. That comparison is not available: of the 243 quarter-hourly days in `recent`, 0 fall in June to September. Quarter-hourly day-ahead has not yet traded a summer, so splitting on the product would measure the season instead. It can be revisited after summer 2027.

The season split is reported instead, which is like-for-like on both counts and is the comparison the regime question actually needs.

| window | season | days | fixed_shape_seasonal capture | ordinary days |

| crisis | Jun-Sep | 244 | 86.6% | 36.1% |
| crisis | Oct-May | 486 | 83.2% | 29.2% |
