# D5 experiment: the 12:00 deadline and the fallback chain

Generated by `python -m src.health.experiments.d5_deadline`. Failures are
injected on 730 validation days with seed 6: weather feed
late on 10% of days, model step failing on
3% and yesterday's prices late on 1%; the seed drew 9.0%, 2.6% and 1.6%. These are
scenario assumptions, not estimates. Late data is retried once after 5
minutes and has not arrived.
Profit is the D1 run's median dispatch P&L for each step, 1 MW / 2 MWh.

## Step runtimes

Median seconds to build the information set and forecast, models fitted,
measured on the machine that ran this experiment; another machine will
differ.

| step | seconds |
|---|---|
| full model | 0.10 |
| model without weather | 0.10 |
| naive previous day | 0.01 |
| seasonal naive previous week | 0.01 |

## Results

| | full model every day | with the chain | without the chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| forecast before the gate | 100.0% | 100.0% | 87.0% |
| median dispatch P&L (€) | 149,498 | 148,660 | 132,762 |
| capture vs perfect foresight | 90.1% | 89.6% | 80.0% |

Fallback days: 95, of which 64 model without weather, 19 naive previous day, 12 seasonal naive previous week.
The latest submission came 10.0 minutes after the issue time.
