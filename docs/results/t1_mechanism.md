# T1 follow-up: how 15:00 to 21:00 loses money

Generated by `python -m src.health.experiments.t1_mechanism` from the saved validation schedules of the production model, lightgbm_conformal: median dispatch against perfect foresight for a 1 MW / 2 MWh battery with €8 wear per MWh discharged, over 730 days from 2024-06-01 to 2026-05-31. "The window" is 15:00 to 20:59 local time. The schedules reproduce the saved daily profit, and the attribution's gaps, to within €0.01. Intervals are 95% moving-block bootstraps over 7-day blocks, 5,000 draws.

## The money

Over the 730 days the gap to perfect foresight is €16,424. The Shapley split puts €6,530 of it in the 15-17 and 18-20 blocks, 39.8% (32.0 to 47.2) of the gap. Forecasts above the realised price carry 10.6% (-1.0 to 20.6) of that window cost and forecasts below it 89.4% (79.4 to 101.0). Sections 2 and 3 share out that window cost. A Shapley cost can be negative, on 136 days here, so a share and its interval can fall outside 0 to 100%.

## Reading

- Inside the window perfect foresight's cash cannot be told from the median schedule's: -3.55 € a day (95% interval -10.02 to +2.87). Even its upper bound is below the €6,530, 8.94 € a day, the Shapley split puts on these hours' forecast errors, so that cost does not show up as cash traded in them.
- Most of the window's cost comes from forecasts below the realised price: 89.4% (79.4 to 101.0).
- Across the day, on point estimates, forecasts below the outcome carry more of the cost in 06-10, 15-17, 18-20 and forecasts above it in 00-05, 11-14, 21-23.
- Perfect foresight sells its window energy at better average prices: the discharge price part is +4.08 € a day (95% interval +2.59 to +6.09), clear of zero in both seasons.
- Perfect foresight buys more energy in the window: the charge volume part is -5.01 € a day (95% interval -7.01 to -3.00), clear of zero October to May.
- Perfect foresight discharges less in the window, saving wear: the wear part is +0.51 € a day (95% interval +0.17 to +0.89), clear of zero June to September.
- These parts cannot be told from zero over all days: discharge volume, charge price.
- Perfect foresight has less energy stored at 15:00 than the median schedule: -0.08 MWh on average (95% interval -0.12 to -0.04).
- Perfect foresight has more energy stored at 21:00 than the median schedule: +0.07 MWh on average (95% interval +0.03 to +0.11).
- Over the whole day perfect foresight's cash comes out ahead beyond noise in 00-05, +4.22 € a day (95% interval +1.27 to +7.09); 11-14, +12.00 € a day (95% interval +5.84 to +18.31); 21-23, +8.06 € a day (95% interval +3.93 to +12.63).
- The timing flag carries the largest share of the window's cost, 44.9% (31.0 to 58.9), but its interval overlaps fewer cycles and emptier, so no single mechanism can be named as the main one.
- The forecast put the window's peak in the right hour on 510 of 730 days, against 478 of 729 for the day before's realised peak hour. Days with the peak an hour or more out carry 41.5% (29.2 to 55.1) of the window's cost.
