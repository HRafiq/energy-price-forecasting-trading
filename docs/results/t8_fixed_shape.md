## The decomposition

* the shape of an ordinary day, with no forecast at all: **84.9%**
* what the production forecast adds on top of it: **+5.2%**
* what nobody reaches, not even perfect foresight's own ceiling: **9.9%**

The best fixed arm is `fixed_shape_seasonal`, at €140,923 against the ceiling's €165,922.

For scale, the `naive_previous_day` forecast, which the backtest table presents as its floor, captures 77.6%. It is a forecast; these arms are not.
