# M2 drift monitor

Generated by `python -m src.health.experiments.m2_drift` from saved
`lightgbm_conformal` forecasts. Nothing refit, the hold-out not rerun.

## Rule

Rolling 28 traded days, pooled over periods. Coverage threshold: the highest value on a 0.5 percentage point grid such that at most 5% of validation days have rolling 90% interval coverage below it. Pinball threshold: rolling mean pinball divided by its validation median; the lowest ratio on a 0.05 grid such that at most 5% of validation days have a ratio above it. Thresholds use validation days only (2024-06-01 to 2026-05-31) and are applied unchanged to the hold-out.
